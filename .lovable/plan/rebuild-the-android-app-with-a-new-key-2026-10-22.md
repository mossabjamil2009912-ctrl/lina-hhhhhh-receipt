# Rebuild the Android app with a new key

## You do
1. Open pwabuilder.com and paste https://first-look-recreated.lovable.app
2. Click Package for stores, then Android, then Generate. Keep the package name app.lovable.first_look_recreated.twa.
3. Download the zip and send me the assetlinks.json file from inside it.
4. Uninstall the old app from your phone, then install the new one.

## I do
1. Put your new assetlinks.json on the site, replacing the old one.
2. Publish, then check the live site serves the new file.

## Result
The app opens full screen with no address bar and shows the newest version. After that, updates show up by themselves.
